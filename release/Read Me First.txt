Thank you for trying Advisor.

■ Installation
After unzipping, drag "Advisor" into your Applications folder in Finder
(or just drop it on the Dock).

■ One-time step before your first launch
macOS blocks apps from unidentified developers from opening normally the
very first time. So just for that first launch, do this instead of a
regular double-click:

  1. Right-click (or Control-click) on Advisor
  2. Choose "Open" from the menu that appears
  3. A warning about an unidentified developer will appear — click "Open"
     again

After that first time, you can just double-click it normally from then on.

■ Getting started
The first time it launches, you'll see a screen to register your API key
(a personal permission code). Just follow the on-screen instructions —
Gemini's free tier lets you get started at no cost.
